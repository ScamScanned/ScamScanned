What it detects

The tricks behind scam listings, spelled out.

Scam storefronts reuse the same handful of plays. ScamScanned watches for the concrete ones — the phrases and page states that show up right before a bad purchase.

Shipping windows that outlast refunds

Third-party sellers list delivery dates weeks past the marketplace’s refund deadline. By the time the item never arrives, buyer protection has already expired.

Delivery in 4–6 weeks Ships from overseas

Burner seller identities

Throwaway storefronts hide behind machine-generated names — garbled strings, random ALL-CAPS words, or fresh importer accounts with no history behind them.

BEST_DEAL_STORE_2024X HUIYANG TRADING CO.

Checkouts that leave the platform

The listing has no buy box. The “purchase” button routes you to an off-site page where marketplace protection, refunds, and dispute tools never apply.

See details on seller’s site

Discounts built to short-circuit you

Implausible markdowns exist to stop you from thinking. An 80–90% “deal” on a mainstream product is the hook — the price is the scam, not the steal.

was $499.99, now $89.99 82% off — today only

How it works

Three steps. Zero configuration.

No accounts, no dashboards, nothing to tune. Install it once and it quietly does its job.

  1. 01

    Runs locally in your browser

    The extension lives in your browser and works on the page in front of it. Nothing about your browsing leaves your machine.

  2. 02

    Scans each page as you shop

    When a product page loads, ScamScanned checks it against its scam heuristics on the spot — shipping terms, seller identity, checkout routing, pricing.

  3. 03

    Shows a warning before checkout

    If a listing trips the signals, a banner appears and names each reason. What you do with the warning is entirely your call.
